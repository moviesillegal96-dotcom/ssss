MLBB Replay Lens

Screen-record a Mobile Legends replay, mark the minimap, and Replay Lens follows both teams through the whole match. It finds teamfights, ganks, invasions, rotations and objective timings, then picks out the habits that repeat.

1Load a replay recording

You're looking at a simulated demo match, so every number below is example data. Choose a video of your own replay to analyze a real game.

How to record a replay that works well
  1. In Mobile Legends open Profile → Battle History (or a saved replay) and play the replay.
  2. Start your phone's screen recorder before the match timer starts. Landscape, 720p or higher.
  3. Keep the minimap visible the whole time. Don't open menus over it. Leave the replay at 1× or 2× and note which.
  4. Trim the draft or loading screen if you can, or set Analyze from in step 2.
  5. All processing happens on your device. The video is never uploaded.
