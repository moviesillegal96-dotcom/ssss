FocusBud

A browser extension that blocks social media once you hit the screen time you chose. The only thing enforcing it is guilt: your plant, Bud.

Try the popup See the block screen Settings

⏱️ Your own limits

A daily total plus a limit for each site. Only time with the tab in front of you counts.

🥀 A plant with feelings

Bud starts every day healthy. Going over your limit or using a guilt pass makes it wilt and cry.

😔 Guilt, not walls

You can always get through, but only after three "are you sure?" questions, a wait that gets longer each time, and typing out a confession.

🎯 Focus sessions

Block every listed site for 25, 50 or 90 minutes with one click.

🔥 Streaks

Days under every limit with zero guilt passes build a streak. Raising your own limits gets guilt-tripped too.

🔒 Private

Everything stays in your browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

Install it (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

  1. Download this focusbud folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the focusbud folder.
  4. Pin FocusBud from the puzzle-piece menu. The settings page opens on its own the first time.

On this demo page nothing is really blocked; your settings are saved in this browser only.
